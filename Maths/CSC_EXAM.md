### 2. Probability of Committee Selection (3 marks)

**Given:** 6 men, 8 women → total 14
**Choose 5 people**

* **i. Exactly 3 men**

  $$
  \text{Ways} = \binom{6}{3} \times \binom{8}{2} = 20 \times 28 = 560 \\
  \text{Total} = \binom{14}{5} = 2002 \\
  P = \frac{560}{2002} = \frac{40}{143}
  $$

  **Answer:** b. $\frac{40}{143}$

* **ii. At least 2 women**

  $$
  \text{Cases: } (2W,3M) + (3W,2M) + (4W,1M) + (5W,0M) = 560 + 840 + 420 + 56 = 1876 \\
  P = \frac{1876}{2002} = \frac{134}{143}
  $$

  **Answer:** a. $\frac{134}{143}$

* **iii. More women than men**

  $$
  \text{Cases: } (3W,2M) + (4W,1M) + (5W,0M) = 840 + 420 + 56 = 1316 \\
  P = \frac{1316}{2002} = \frac{94}{143}
  $$

  **Answer:** c. $\frac{94}{143}$

### 5. Linear Combination of Vectors (2 marks)

Find $y$ so that $(1, y, 5)$ is in span of $(1, -3, 2)$, $(2, -1, 1)$

Solution:

$$
c_1 = 3, c_2 = -1 \Rightarrow y = -3(3) - (-1) = -8
$$

**Answer:** c. $y = -8$

### 14. Limit and Parameter (2 marks)

$$
\lim_{x \to -\infty} \left( \frac{a + \frac{1}{x}}{a - \frac{1}{x}} \right)^{-x} = e^{-1} \\

$$

**Answer:** b. 2

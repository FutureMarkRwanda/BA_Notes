## 📘 Topic: **Study Completely a Function**

### ✳️ Definition:

To **study completely** a function means to analyze and describe its **entire behavior**, including:

* Domain
* Symmetry
* Intercepts
* Limits
* Asymptotes
* Derivatives (monotonicity, local extrema)
* Concavity and inflection points
* Graph sketching

---

## 🧮 Elements of the Complete Study:

### 1. **Domain of Definition**

* Find all values of $x$ for which the function is defined.
* Example: For $f(x) = \frac{1}{x-2}$, the domain is $\mathbb{R} \setminus \{2\}$

---

### 2. **Parity and Symmetry**

* Check if the function is **even**, **odd**, or **neither**.

  * Even: $f(-x) = f(x)$ → symmetric about the **y-axis**.
  * Odd: $f(-x) = -f(x)$ → symmetric about the **origin**.

---

### 3. **Intercepts**

* **x-intercepts**: Solve $f(x) = 0$
* **y-intercept**: Find $f(0)$

---

### 4. **Limits and Continuity**

* Compute limits at critical points (e.g. endpoints, undefined points, infinities).
* Identify **discontinuities** and **asymptotes**.

  * Vertical asymptotes: where the function goes to $\pm \infty$
  * Horizontal asymptotes: $\lim_{x \to \infty} f(x)$, $\lim_{x \to -\infty} f(x)$

---

### 5. **First Derivative – Monotonicity**

* Compute $f'(x)$
* Determine:

  * Intervals of **increase/decrease**
  * **Critical points** where $f'(x) = 0$
  * **Local minima and maxima** (via sign chart or First Derivative Test)

---

### 6. **Second Derivative – Concavity**

* Compute $f''(x)$
* Determine:

  * Intervals of **concave up / concave down**
  * **Inflection points** (where concavity changes)

---

### 7. **Asymptotes**

* **Vertical**: Where the function is undefined and tends to $\pm\infty$
* **Horizontal** or **Oblique**: Behavior as $x \to \pm\infty$

---

### 8. **Sketching the Graph**

* Combine all information:

  * Domain
  * Intercepts
  * Asymptotes
  * Monotonicity
  * Concavity
  * Key points
* Draw an accurate graph showing this behavior

---

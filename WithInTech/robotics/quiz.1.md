**10. A robot circuit has a motor rated at 6V and requires 0.5A. What resistor value would limit the current from a 9V battery to this level (use V = IR)?**
A) 4.5 Ω
B) 6 Ω ✅
C) 3 Ω
D) 12 Ω

> 🧠 *Explanation:* Voltage to drop: 9V - 6V = 3V
> V = IR ⇒ 3 = R × 0.5 ⇒ **R = 6 Ω**

## 🛠️ **Bonus: Scenario-Based Calculation Problem**

**Problem:**

You're designing a small robot to move forward using a DC motor that requires **0.25 A** at **5V**. You only have a **9V battery**. What resistor value should you add to safely run the motor?

A) 8 Ω
B) 12 Ω
✅ **C) 16 Ω**
D) 20 Ω

> **Solution:**
> Voltage to drop = 9V - 5V = 4V
> Current = 0.25 A
> V = IR ⇒ 4 = R × 0.25 → **R = 16 Ω**
